Overview

Descend into the heart of Ancient Rome on this exclusive underground tour of the Colosseum. While most visitors only see the arena floor from above, you'll walk through the dark tunnels where gladiators prepared for battle and wild animals were kept in cages before being hoisted into the sunlight.

Following your subterranean exploration, walk the footsteps of emperors through the Roman Forum and climb the Palatine Hill for breathtaking views of the eternal city. This small-group experience ensures personalized attention from your expert historian guide.
