Expert Travel Tips: Historical Sites

schedule

Book Early Access

The most famous sites get crowded by 10 AM. Look for 'Early Morning' or 'First Entry' tours to enjoy the sites in peace.

check_circle

Check Dress Codes

Many religious historical sites (like the Vatican or Temples in Egypt) require shoulders and knees to be covered.

hiking

Wear Proper Shoes

Archaeological sites often have uneven, ancient stones. Sturdy walking shoes are better than sandals.
