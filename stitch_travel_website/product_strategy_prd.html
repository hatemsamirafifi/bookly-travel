# Global Tours Marketplace - Product Requirements & Design Strategy ## 1. Project Overview A premium, multi-vendor travel marketplace for tours, featuring direct booking, multi-language/currency support, and comprehensive management dashboards for partners and admins. ## 2. Brand Identity & Visual Language * **Aesthetic:** Modern, premium, scalable, and trustworthy. * **Color Palette:** * **Primary:** Deep Blue / Navy (#0A2540) * **Accent:** Warm Gold / Soft Amber (#FFB800) * **Base:** White / Off-white (#F8FAFC) * **Neutrals:** Clean Gray Neutrals (#64748B) * **Typography:** Polished modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans). * **UI Style:** Subtle shadows, rounded corners (8-12px), soft spacing, and a clean grid. ## 3. Core Modules & Screen List ### Phase 1: Public Website (Guest Experience) * **Homepage:** Hero search, featured destinations, popular categories, featured tours, value props, testimonials, partner CTA, blog section. * **Tours Listing:** Advanced filtering (price, duration, rating, category), sort controls, high-impact tour cards. * **Tour Detail:** Image gallery, booking widget, highlights, inclusions, logistics, reviews, FAQ. * **Destination & Category Pages:** Curated views for specific locations or tour types. * **Blog/Article:** Travel inspiration and guides. * **Auth:** Premium Login/Register flows. ### Phase 2: Customer Area (Post-Booking) * **Dashboard:** Booking overview, personalized recommendations. * **Bookings:** List and detail views with status tracking. * **Wishlist & Profile:** Managing saved tours and account preferences. ### Phase 3: Partner Dashboard (Vendor Management) * **Dashboard:** KPI cards (Revenue, Bookings, Rating), performance charts. * **Tour Management:** Listing, creating, and editing tours with pricing and availability slots. * **Operations:** Booking management, review responses, earnings tracking. * **Onboarding:** Professional flow for new partners. ### Phase 4: Admin Dashboard (Marketplace Governance) * **Moderation:** Approval workflows for partners and tours. * **Management:** Global booking and review management. * **System:** Platform settings and CMS for public content. ## 4. Key UX Requirements * **Global Elements:** Multilingual (LTR/RTL ready) and multi-currency selectors in the header. * **Search & Discovery:** Robust filtering and card-based discovery. * **SaaS-Grade Dashboards:** Professional sidebar navigation, data tables, and KPI summaries. * **Conversion Focus:** Clear, persistent booking widgets and CTAs.
